## XY (charting library) — build-time dependency

`xy==0.0.7` (https://github.com/reflex-dev/xy, PyPI `xy`) is used only to generate the
corpus (`xybench build`, `xybench verify --regenerate`). It is not vendored or
redistributed. Its licence is Apache-2.0 (per its distribution metadata and bundled
LICENSE). The SVG files under `data/frozen/` are XY's exported output for synthetic data
generated by this repository, with marker ids inserted and, in the masked variant,
coordinate values replaced; they carry no XY-authored content beyond the serialisation.
